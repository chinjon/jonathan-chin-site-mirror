right now, being [anti-ai is cool and normal](https://www.wheresyoured.at/let-ai-burn/), but [maybe it won’t stay that way forever](https://activisthandbook.org/wellbeing/burnout). in the year 2026, it is without question, everyone hates ai for all sorts of reasons; electricity, employment, ram, climate change, and pile in whatever you want for good measure. anger and bitterness is fomenting as companies douse all of their resources in kerosene for the ai build out that is not paying off.

late 2016/early 2017 feels like 20 years ago, [the “#resistance”](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2017/apr/24/100-days-of-trump-resistance-wins-so-far-battles-to-come) felt like it would have momentum all the way through 2020 and beyond. but it didn’t, in 2024, many of the voting groups that went blue, [saw cracks](https://www.pewresearch.org/politics/2025/06/26/voting-patterns-in-the-2024-election/). in 2024, it was obviously not still all there. the “#resistance” eventually fizzled out, even “#nokings” has to some [extent as well](https://activisthandbook.org/wellbeing/burnout), [protest fatigue is a real thing](https://theowp.org/the-rising-threat-of-protest-fatigue/).

but the fight against ai won’t be in big cities or online, it’ll be in rural areas and small communities, where the data centers have the water and space they seek. farmers and small property owners are not just saying “no”, they’re making a stick about it, because its not only their lively hoods that are being threatened, their literal place of living are being seized. while [we dunk on](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c9wx2dz2v44o) [ai companies](https://microslop.com/) and [the slop](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/microslop) that come out, we'll need to keep it human and not let it devolve into a low-stakes meme reference.
