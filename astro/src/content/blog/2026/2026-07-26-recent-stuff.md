### the odyssey

i saw "the odyssey" in the lesser regular format. it was good, but loud. a bit more fast paced than other of nolan's works? it was a bit chaotic the first 30 minutes with the establishing of the story, but it smoothed out to close out the first hour. i am still on my own odyssey to catch it in 70mm imax at lincoln square, but that may take 10 years...

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4mKSQkAoTEw 

### "spontaneous music live" by sml

i stumbled upon this record on bandcamp (only to find out about the glowing p4k review later in day) and woah. blown away with the audio engineering. it live but it sounds like a studio recording. the entire 40 minutes teases a frenetic build up that never putters out, but keeps up a controlled kinetic energy that grooves.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r1Dw2u0uK2s
