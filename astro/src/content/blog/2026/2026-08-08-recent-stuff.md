### [watch] the prisoner (1967)

been watching "the prisoner", a british series created by patrick mcgoohan (who later went on to collaborate on "columbo"). it's a kafkaesque show that inspired "twin peaks", "the truman show", and any other piece of media with opaque plots and weird vibes.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pCOyRuDe36I 

### [tech] koreader sync

finally found a way to sync up reading progress from other devices with my xteink x4 ereader. the crosspoint team has been working on getting [koreader](https://koreader.rocks/) via [crosspoint sync](https://sync.crosspointreader.com/). i found [readest](https://readest.com/) which has a suite of apps including for linux, ios, and web browsers, can sync across all devices.
